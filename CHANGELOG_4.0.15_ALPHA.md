# Legion RX 4.0.15 Alpha — Track Day & Race Control

- Added fourth discipline card: Track Day.
- Track Day Free Practice sessions: 10/30/60/120 min.
- Track Day uses LapWiz Free Practice continuously and stores reports separately from championships.
- Participants ranked primarily by completed laps, then by best lap.
- Session-best lap shown prominently.
- Driver rows show laps, best lap and last lap.
- Tap a driver to open complete lap history with Best / Average / Worst metrics and highlighting.
- Race live lap history is now collected for current RallyCross heats too.
- Added Race Management control in the cockpit:
  - skip 1/2/3/5 upcoming heats,
  - cancel current qualification round,
  - finish qualification early,
  - finish the competition early.
- Cancelled events are marked in the event flow.
- Audio layer remains based on the working 4.0.14 implementation.
