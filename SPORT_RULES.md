# LEGION RX — CURRENT SPORT RULES · RC63

## RallyCross

Unchanged by RC63. RallyCross keeps its own independent ruleset and Final A tie-break agreed previously: counted BEST-2 sporting results, then discarded third result, then RUN-OFF on absolute equality; no hidden time comparison.

## Classic RC · EFRA 2026 Round-by-Round

Unchanged from RC62. Classic RC owns Practice/Seeding, balanced heats up to 10, Round-by-Round qualifying points, automatic counted qualifying rounds, A/B/C finals, three final legs / BEST 2 and EFRA tie-breaks.

RC63 Director actions do **not** alter the scoring formulas. A deliberately skipped scored heat records DNS for its entered pilots so the progression remains explicit instead of fabricating performance. Operational current-heat duration/min-lap overrides are event-control overrides, not a change to the EFRA scoring engine.

Schedule pauses, early start, break adjustment and competition hold/resume affect only the neutral timeline.
